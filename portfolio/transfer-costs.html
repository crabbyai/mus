The Price Is Never The Price

What it really costs to transfer

Between shaking hands on a number and holding the file, you pay advance income tax, stamp duty, a registration fee, capital value or town tax depending on where you are, the society’s own transfer schedule, and the agent. Most buyers find this out in the week they can least afford to.

Every rate here is read from a single data file that is checked against FBR and the provincial boards on a schedule, and this page tells you the date it was last confirmed. It is still a guide, not advice: valuations for tax are assessed on the DC or FBR table rather than what you actually paid, societies set their own schedules, and your own filing history changes the answer. Ask me to price your specific transaction before you commit to it.
