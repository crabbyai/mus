    The Person Behind the Performance

    I walk into a room and it warms up.

    I'm Adeel Rahman. People tell me I'm the one they remember from the dinner — the easy laugh, the story that lands, the friend who notices when someone's gone quiet. I'm generous with my attention and stubborn about my standards, and I've never met a stranger I couldn't get talking in five minutes. That is the instrument I bring to a set.

    Under the charm there's a worker. I hold an MBA from Carleton University in Canada and spent years across the table in high-stakes negotiations, which is its own acting school: you learn to listen harder than you speak, to read what a person isn't saying, and to hold your nerve when the room goes silent. I turn up early, off-book and in a good mood — and I stay that way at hour fourteen.

    Good to Know

    Questions people always ask

    For casting directors — and for everyone here for the dramas.

    What kind of work are you available for?

    Drama serials, feature and short film, television commercials, web series, theatre and voice-over, in Karachi and Lahore. Lead, supporting or a single strong scene — I care more about the writing than the size of the part.

    Where are you from?

    I was born in Canada and I work in both Urdu and English. That mix is the point: I can play the man who never left Lahore and the one who just flew in from Toronto, and the camera believes both.

    Why cast a businessman with an MBA?

    Because the business of a shoot is time and money, and I understand both. I arrive prepared, I don't cost you retakes, and I play professionals, power and money from experience rather than imitation. My MBA from Carleton University isn't a line on a wall; it's how I approach every call sheet.

    What are your rates?

    Quoted per project once I've read the script and schedule — per day or per episode, agreed in writing before the shoot. No surprises later.

    Can you do outdoor schedules and long shoot days?

    Yes. I'm available in Karachi and Lahore and can travel for outdoor schedules. Long days are part of the job; I keep the same energy on the last take as the first.

    Can I see headshots, a showreel or a self-tape?

    Yes. Message me on WhatsApp with the project and I'll send current headshots and reel material, or record a self-tape of your sides.

    Where can I watch every episode of Parizaad or Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum?

    Right here, in the drama library. Open the serial, pick an episode number and it plays on this page from the broadcaster's official YouTube upload — Parizaad from HUM TV, Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum from ARY Digital. Your place is remembered, so next time it offers to continue where you stopped.

    How quickly do new episodes appear?

    The feed re-reads the official ARY Digital, HUM TV, Har Pal Geo, Green Entertainment and Express TV channels every hour. A new episode shows up within about an hour of the channel uploading it, and a brand-new serial is added by itself once its first episodes are out.

    Are you in these dramas? Is it legal to watch them here?

    I'm not in them — they're the work I watch and learn from. Nothing is copied or re-uploaded: each episode is YouTube's own player showing the channel's own video, so the broadcaster keeps the views and the advertising. If a channel removes an episode, it disappears here too.
