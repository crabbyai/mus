Questions

Is this Islamic?

Yes. Adeel Ahmed Rahman is a Muslim, nothing more and nothing less. Every practice comes from the Qur'an and Sunnah: dhikr, dua, istikhara, the morning and evening adhkar, tawakkul. "Manifestation" here simply means dua.

Do I have to be Muslim?

No. It's open to everyone, of every background. The focus is on people who are already Muslim, so it's taught in the language of the Qur'an and Sunnah. If you're curious about Islam, you're very welcome: start with the free course and ask anything in the forum.

I'm a doctor / lawyer / therapist / first responder. Is this for me?

Yes. Many people in demanding, caring work carry a lot and rarely ask for help. The lessons are short, and you can ask questions in the forum whenever you have a moment.

Is this therapy or medical advice?

No. Coaching works alongside therapy and medical care, never instead of it. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (Canada and US) or your local emergency number first.
