Angel number 622 · The year of the Hijra

In 622 CE, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ left Makkah for Madinah. Years of hardship ended, and a community, a home and a new life began. It was such a turning point that the Muslim calendar counts every year from it.

So when 622 keeps showing up for you, on a clock, a receipt or a licence plate, take it as a reminder, not a fortune. Your Hijra is waiting: from the life you're stuck in to the life you're asking Allah for. Leave the old self behind in Makkah. Walk toward Madinah.

In the cave on the way, with the trackers steps away, the Prophet ﷺ told Abu Bakr:

لَا تَحْزَنْ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ مَعَنَا“Do not grieve; indeed Allah is with us.”Qur'an 9:40

Angel numbers, the Muslim way

People call repeating numbers “angel numbers”. As Sunni Muslims we don't read fortunes in them; only Allah knows the unseen. But a repeating number can be a beautiful nudge to stop and remember Him. Here's how I use them with my clients:

Manifestation is just dua

  • Ask. Allah says ٱدْعُونِىٓ أَسْتَجِبْ لَكُمْ, “Call upon Me; I will respond to you.” (40:60)
  • Expect good. In the hadith qudsi: “I am as My servant thinks of Me.” (Bukhari & Muslim)
  • Remember. فَٱذْكُرُونِىٓ أَذْكُرْكُمْ, “Remember Me; I will remember you.” (2:152)
  • Change within. “Allah does not change the condition of a people until they change what is in themselves.” (13:11)
