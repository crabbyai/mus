Before You Sign

What the plan actually costs you

Almost nothing here is bought outright. Everyone works out whether they can manage the down payment and stops. The month that catches people is the one where a quarterly instalment and a half-yearly payment land together. Lay the whole thing out and look at it.

Instalments

Each instalment—
First twelve months—the number that decides it
Heaviest single month—
Paid in total—across the whole plan

Every figure here is your own input and plain arithmetic — nothing is assumed about any particular society’s terms. Real plans vary in how they treat development charges, possession dues and early settlement, so treat this as the shape of the commitment and let me check the actual schedule before you sign anything.
