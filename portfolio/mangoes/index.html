The Varieties

Premium mangoes, Pakistani summers

Chaunsa, Sindhri, Dasehri, Langra, Anwar Ratol, Falsa and Jamun, plus Pakistani vegetables. Tap one to add it to your order.

Earliest

Sindhri

Late May – July

Large, smooth and honey-sweet with very little fibre. The mango that opens the season.

Small & intense

Anwar Ratol

June – July

Small, fragrant and intensely sweet. The connoisseur's favourite — a short season, so it goes fast.

Tangy-sweet

Langra

June – July

Green-skinned even when ripe, with a bright, tangy sweetness and a perfume all its own.

Multan's pride

Chaunsa

July – early September

Rich, aromatic and almost fibre-free — the mango Multan is famous for, and the one most people mean when they say "Pakistani mango".

Slender & sweet

Dasehri

June – July

Long, slim and fibre-free with a soft, sugary flesh. A North Indian and Punjabi classic.

Summer berry

Jamun

June – July

The deep-purple Java plum: sweet, tart and a little astringent. Best with a pinch of chaat masala.

Rare treat

Falsa

May – June

Tiny, tangy-sweet berries for the famous falsa sharbat. A taste of Pakistani summers you can't find in Canada.

When Your Box Arrives

Ripen, store, enjoy

🌡️ Room temperature first

Open the box and keep the mangoes out of direct sun. Don't refrigerate them until they're ripe — cold stops the ripening.

👃 Trust your nose

A ripe mango smells sweet at the stem and gives slightly when pressed. Colour alone can mislead — Langra stays green.

❄️ Then chill

Once ripe, the fridge keeps them a few more days. Bring to room temperature before eating for the full flavour.

🥤 Too many?

Peel, cube and freeze for lassi, kulfi and shakes all year. Nobody has ever had too many Chaunsa.

Questions

Good to know

When will I get my mangoes?

In season — roughly late May to early September, depending on the variety you choose and the weather that year. We'll confirm a shipping window for your city before anything is charged.

How much does a box cost?

Prices depend on the variety, the box size and the country, and are set once the season's harvest and air-freight rates are known. Reserve now and you'll get the price first — no obligation.

Do I pay now?

No. A pre-order reserves your place in the queue. You confirm and pay only when we send your price and delivery window.

Will they arrive ripe?

They're picked mature but firm so they survive the journey, and ripen at room temperature within a few days of arriving. See "When your box arrives" above.

Is it allowed to import Pakistani mangoes into my country?

Each country has its own rules for fresh fruit, and every shipment is prepared to meet them — paperwork and any required treatment included. If your country's rules change in a season, we'll tell you before you pay.

Can I order for a shop or restaurant?

Yes — choose "Wholesale / business" in the form and tell us roughly how much you'd like per week.
