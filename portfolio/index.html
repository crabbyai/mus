Good to Know

Questions buyers always ask

Straight answers before we ever speak. Anything else — message me on WhatsApp.

Do you work with overseas Pakistanis who can't visit in person?

Constantly — it's a large part of my practice. I run live video walkthroughs of every viewing, share verified documents digitally, and can complete a purchase or sale on a registered power of attorney. Several homes in this portfolio were closed entirely for clients in the UK, Gulf and North America who never set foot inside until they had the keys.

Which areas and plot sizes do you cover?

Premium residential across Islamabad and Lahore — DHA, Bahria Town, Gulberg Greens, F-7, E-11, B-17 and Model Town among them. I handle everything from 5 Marla starter homes to 2 Kanal estates and farmhouses, with off-market access to inventory that never reaches the public listings.

How is your commission structured?

Standard market rate of 1% from each side on a completed transaction, agreed in writing before we begin. There are no upfront charges and no fee for valuations or consultations — I'm paid only when your deal closes successfully.

Can you arrange home financing or a bank loan?

Yes. I work with home-finance teams at several major banks and can connect you directly for pre-approval. The payment planner above gives you an indicative monthly figure; the bank confirms exact markup and tenure based on your profile.

Do you handle the legal vetting, transfer and possession?

End to end. Title verification, fard and mutation checks, dues clearance, transfer at the society or registrar, and physical possession — all managed and overseen on your behalf so nothing is left to chance.

How long does a typical sale take?

It depends on pricing and location, but the homes on this wall averaged just over three weeks from listing to handover — several closed in under two. Correct pricing and a ready buyer pool are what move a property fast, and that's where most of my work happens before the sign ever goes up.
